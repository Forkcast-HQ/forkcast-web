ForkcastProduct
The moat

The nutrition layer competitors can’t buy off the shelf.

Chain-exact data

USDA FoodData Central + Nutritionix / FatSecret cover the 20+-location chains the FDA already forces to disclose. Table stakes — available to anyone.

Independent-restaurant estimation our moat

No commercial database has nutrition for independent restaurants. We estimate it from menu name + description + photo, RAG-grounded against trusted data (cuts error 63–83%), with confidence always shown — never “clinical.”

Every user correction and partner-supplied menu compounds the data advantage — a moat a vision model alone can’t replicate.

Business model

Three revenue legs, sequenced.

Launch leg

Consumer subscription

~$60/yr blended, annual-led to fight churn. Transparent pricing + a useful free tier. Gate growth on LTV:CAC ≥ 3:1.

Margin scaler

Restaurant retail-media

Clearly-labeled “featured healthy dish” at ~$200/mo/location. 10,000 locations ≈ $24M. DoorDash’s $1B+ ad model, ~90% margin.

Durable moat

B2B2C covered nutrition

Payer/employer contracts on reimbursable medical nutrition therapy (CPT 97802–04) — billable day one, “free to the user.”
