\section{How we earn money --- three streams, switched on in order}

\subsection*{\textcolor{accent}{1.} Diner subscriptions — from day one}
The core experience is \textbf{free forever}: Fit Scores, discovery, ordering, and confirmed meal logging. Premium sells the personal science --- adaptive calorie calibration, pattern analytics, unlimited photo AI:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lrr}
\toprule
 & \textbf{Monthly} & \textbf{Annual}\\
\midrule
\textbf{Forkcast Premium} & \textbf{\$4.99} & \textbf{\$39.99}\\
MyFitnessPal Premium (for comparison) & \$19.99 & \$79.99\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Both billing options exist; we steer to \textbf{annual} (it roughly halves churn). We win every price comparison on entry, and pricing headroom is upward as verified trust earns it.

\subsection*{\textcolor{accent}{2.} Restaurant order commission — as volume proves out}
\textbf{6\% only on pickup orders that start inside Forkcast.} The fee is not for delivery --- it pays for the \emph{customer}: a health-driven diner we steered to that restaurant, tracked cleanly because the order flows through our checkout. DoorDash charges the same 6\% on its pickup orders, so the price is already market-validated --- except with us, verification tools, the order terminal, and allergy flags are \textbf{free}, and a diner who walks in without ordering through the app costs the restaurant nothing.

\subsection*{\textcolor{accent}{3.} Featured placement \& employer/insurer programs — later}
Restaurants already receiving orders can buy clearly-labeled featured slots (from \$99/mo) --- which \textbf{never affect Fit Scores}. In later years, employers and insurers pay for covered nutrition programs. Both layer on top; neither is required for the model to work.

\section{Funding, simply}

\textbf{Raising $\sim$\$1.0--1.5M pre-seed} to run the Boston pilot, sign the first verified restaurant partners, and prove retention --- followed by a seed round ($\sim$\$5M) once those numbers exist, and roughly \$8M total before the business sustains itself. Government small-business research grants (SBIR) stack on top without dilution.

\vspace{6pt}
\textbf{Discipline rules:} growth spending stops whenever a subscriber cohort's economics fall below 3:1 value-to-cost; the restaurant fee stays performance-only until order volume earns more; and no revenue stream ever compromises the product's core promise --- sponsorship can never change a Fit Score.
